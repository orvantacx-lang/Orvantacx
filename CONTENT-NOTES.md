# Content notes: claims deliberately not made, and items for your decision

## Not on the site, on purpose
No client names or logos, testimonials, case studies, ratings or review markup, statistics, years of experience, employee counts, revenue, partnerships, certifications, awards, UK/US/foreign offices, registered address or registration number, WhatsApp or social links, live dashboards, call activity or "live" AI demos. No "unlimited", "24/7" or guaranteed-results wording. Price figures are only those you supplied, in rupees, with no currency conversion.

The only company facts used are those in your brief: name, tagline, Lucknow (city/state/country only), email, phone, languages, enquiry hours, service coverage.

## Illustrative material (labelled as such on the page)
- The hero illustration is abstract; its dashboard tile is captioned "Summaries (illustrative)".
- The English/Hindi conversation on the AI Voice Solutions page is a written design sample, labelled "Illustrative example only".
- Hindi sample text should be read by a fluent speaker before launch.

## Decisions for you
1. **Enquiry hours** appear as business enquiry hours and are explicitly not a human-support promise. Extended AI coverage is described only as configurable and subject to agreement.
2. **Starting prices** (₹15,000 / ₹35,000 / ₹60,000+) are shown exactly as supplied, with the disclaimer. Confirm you are comfortable publishing them.
3. **The three extra services** (AI Customer Support, Lead Qualification and Appointment Booking, Call Analytics and Reporting) are described in full on the Services page, not on separate pages, because your page list has 14 pages.
4. **The 4th card on the home page, "AI Contact Centre Management"**, links to How We Work (it is a managed approach, not one of the six services).
5. **Legal**: Privacy Policy and Terms are marked on-page as drafts. Bracketed items (`[... to be confirmed during legal review]`) need a lawyer: retention periods, transfer mechanisms, payment terms, liability limits, governing law.
6. **Healthcare**: the Industries page states that no diagnosis, medical advice, treatment recommendation or emergency triage is offered. Keep it.
7. **Outbound calling** wording requires authorisation, consent, preference checks and telecom compliance, and says there is no unsolicited mass calling. Keep it consistent with how you actually operate.
8. Footer shows the current year automatically at build time (© 2026 now).

## Accessibility colour note
The brand teal `#54C7B4` and grey `#697B90` do not reach WCAG AA contrast for small text on white. Large areas and dark backgrounds use the exact brand colours; small text on white uses a darker teal `#17776a` and grey `#52657a`. This is the only deliberate departure from the supplied palette.

## Known limitation: Content-Security-Policy
`public/_headers` sets a strict CSP, but it allows `'unsafe-inline'` for scripts and styles because Astro inlines small scripts/styles. Moving to hashes/nonces is possible later; it is not needed for the site to function.
